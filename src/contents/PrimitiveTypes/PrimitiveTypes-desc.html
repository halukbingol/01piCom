  • Declare variable a as int. The content of a is unknown, which is represented by u, since it is not initialized yet.

  • Assign the literal 4 to a. The content of a becomes 4.

  • Declaration and initialization can be done at once. Declare variable b as int and assign 7 to it at one step.

  • Change the content of b to 8
